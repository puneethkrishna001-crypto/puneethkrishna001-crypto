**Tech Stack:** `Python` | `XGBoost` | `SHAP` | `SQL` | `Power BI` | `Streamlit` | `Scikit-learn`

> **Problem:** Identify high-risk customers before they churn and enable targeted retention strategies for a telecom company.

**🏆 Technical Achievements:**
- ✅ Built **XGBoost ensemble model** achieving **83%+ ROC-AUC** on **7K+ telecom records**

- ✅ Cross-validation F1-score: **0.78** | Precision: **0.81** | Recall: **0.75**
- ✅ Built interactive **Power BI dashboard** with:
  - Real-time churn probability tracking
  - Revenue at risk quantification
  - Customer segmentation by risk tier
  - Retention recommendation engine

**📈 Business Impact:**
- 💰 Identified **1,200+ high-risk customers** (17% of customer base)
- 💼 Estimated retention potential: **$500K+ annual revenue protection**
- ⚡ Enabled proactive intervention campaigns
- 📊 Reduced churn prediction errors by **22%** vs. baseline heuristics
